# Video Speech Translator & Transcriber

An automated video transcription and translation pipeline powered by OpenAI's Whisper model. It extracts audio from video files, transcribes spoken content (e.g., Hindi), and generates English translations or transcripts.

## Features
- Transcribes Hindi and multilingual video audio into Devanagari or English text.
- Processes local video files (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.mkv`).
- Powered by OpenAI Whisper's `medium` model.

## Prerequisites
Ensure you have `ffmpeg` installed on your system.
